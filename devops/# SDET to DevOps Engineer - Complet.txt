## Tips for Success

### Daily Habits
1. **Consistent Learning:** Stick to your 2-hour daily commitment
2. **Hands-on Practice:** Always implement what you learn
3. **Documentation:** Keep detailed notes and create how-to guides
4. **Community Engagement:** Join DevOps communities and forums

### Weekly Reviews
- Assess what you learned
- Identify knowledge gaps
- Adjust next week's focus if needed
- Update your learning journal

### Monthly Projects
- Build something substantial each month
- Share your work on GitHub
- Write blog posts about your learnings
- Get feedback from the DevOps community

## Job Preparation Strategy

### Resume Enhancement
- Highlight transferable skills from SDET role

## Timeline Summary
- **Months 1-2:** Foundation and core tools mastery
- **Months 3-4:** Advanced practices and cloud integration
- **Months 5-6:** Specialization and portfolio development
- **Total Investment:** 6 months × 30 days × 2 hours = 360 hours

This roadmap leverages your existing SDET and Python skills while systematically building the comprehensive skill set needed for a successful DevOps career. The key is consistent daily practice and hands-on implementation of everything you learn.
